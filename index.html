My short story

Building vision models
where medicine meets machine learning.

I'm a second-year Information Systems student in Egypt and a machine learning engineer focused on computer vision. Most of my work is on medical images: chest and dental X-rays, breast ultrasound and skin lesions. I spend a lot of time on the parts that make a number believable, then I turn working models into things people can use, like a dental X-ray app or the deepcsv library.
